# 🚀 NamaSoft - All-in-One POS & ERP Solution
## One Platform to Run Your Entire Business

---

## Why NamaSoft?

NamaSoft is a cloud-based, all-in-one business management platform that combines **Point of Sale**, **Accounting**, **Inventory Management**, and **Human Resources** into a single, easy-to-use system. Built specifically for the Saudi Arabian market with full ZATCA e-invoicing compliance.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 💰 Point of Sale (POS)
- Fast, intuitive sales interface
- Instant barcode scanning
- Smart search by name or code
- Multiple payment methods: Cash, Card, Credit
- Invoice creation in seconds
- Direct thermal receipt printing
- Works on desktop, mobile, and tablet

### 📦 Product Management
- Support for **10,000+ products** with zero lag
- Multi-level categories and classifications
- Unique barcode for each product
- Separate buy and sell prices
- Automatic stock tracking with every transaction
- Low stock alerts and minimum quantity warnings
- Sell-by-weight support
- Bilingual product data (Arabic & English)

### 🧾 ZATCA E-Invoicing Compliance
- **100% compliant with Saudi tax authority requirements**
- Phase 1 & Phase 2 support
- Certified QR Code on every invoice
- Automatic digital signing with Production CSID certificate
- Real-time invoice reporting to Fatoora platform
- Automatic 15% VAT calculation

### 📊 Smart Dashboard
- Real-time daily statistics: Sales, Purchases, Profit
- Interactive charts for performance analysis
- Current treasury balance at a glance
- Product count and low stock warnings
- Complete business health overview in one screen

### 💳 Comprehensive Financial Management
- **Treasury:** Automatic tracking of all cash flow (income/expenses)
- **Expenses:** Categorize and track all company expenses
- **Installments:** Flexible payment plans with automatic scheduling
- Automatic sales-to-treasury linking

### 📚 Full Double-Entry Accounting
- Pre-built chart of accounts (41 accounts), fully customizable
- Automatic journal entries with every sale
- **Trial Balance** - Verify account accuracy
- **Balance Sheet** - Complete financial position
- **Income Statement** - Revenue vs expenses analysis
- **General Ledger** - Detailed account history
- Balanced and accurate accounting records

### 👥 Customer & Supplier Management
- Comprehensive customer and supplier database
- Complete tax details (name, VAT number, address)
- Customer balance and credit limit tracking
- Automatic invoice-to-customer linking

### 🏪 Advanced Inventory Management
- Multi-warehouse support
- Inter-warehouse stock transfers
- Physical inventory count vs system comparison
- Full stock movement tracking (in/out/transfer/adjustment)
- Detailed inventory reports

### 👨‍💼 Human Resources Management
- Complete employee records
- Daily attendance check-in/check-out system
- Monthly payroll processing (basic + allowances - deductions)
- Employee leave management (annual, sick, emergency)

### 📋 Complete Business Operations
- **Price Quotes** - Professional quotations for clients
- **Purchase Orders** - Organized supplier ordering
- **Sales Returns** - Easy customer return processing
- **Purchase Returns** - Supplier return management
- **Bookings** - Flexible reservation system
- **Maintenance** - Device repair request tracking

### 🎁 Loyalty & Promotions System
- **Loyalty Points** with tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
- Multiple promotion types:
  - Buy One Get One Free (BOGO)
  - Percentage discount
  - Fixed amount discount
  - Happy Hour deals
- **Customizable discount coupons**
- **Gift cards** with reloadable balance

### 🤖 Smart Telegram Bot
- Add expenses by simply sending a Telegram message
- Record purchases instantly from your phone
- Real-time notifications
- Perfect for managing on-the-go

### 📸 OCR Invoice Scanning
- Photograph supplier invoices and add them automatically
- Smart extraction of product names, prices, and quantities
- Save hours of manual data entry
- High-accuracy text recognition

### 📱 Works on All Devices
- Fully responsive design
- Works on desktop, mobile, and tablet
- Mobile-optimized POS with large touch targets
- Safe Area support for notched devices

### 📈 Comprehensive Reports
- Daily and monthly sales reports
- Purchase and expense reports
- Full accounting reports
- Export data to Excel
- Download reports as PDF

### 🔐 Enterprise-Grade Security
- Encrypted passwords (bcrypt hashing)
- Multi-level user permissions (Admin, Cashier, Accountant)
- Complete audit trail for all operations
- Sensitive data protection
