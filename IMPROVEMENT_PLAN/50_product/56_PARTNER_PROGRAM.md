## 🎯 الخطة

### 56.1 — Partner Tiers (3 أيام)
| Tier | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Referrer** | عميل مكتسب | 10% (3 months) |
| **Reseller** | 5+ عملاء | 20% recurring |
| **Implementation** | 10+ + certified | 30% + leads |
| **Strategic** | 50+ | Custom + co-marketing |

### 56.2 — Partner Portal (10 أيام)
- partners.namasoft.com
- Track referred deals
- Commission earnings
- Marketing materials library
- Lead submission
- Resources & training

### 56.3 — Certified Accountant Program (8 أيام)
- Online certification course
- Exam (proctored)
- Listed on namasoft.com/find-accountant
- Earn 30% commission per referral
- Annual recertification

### 56.4 — Commission Engine (5 أيام)
- Track per-deal commissions
- Recurring (per subscription cycle)
- Tiered rates
- Auto-payments (monthly)
- Tax compliance (1099 / IRS analog for Saudi)

### 56.5 — Referral Tracking (4 أيام)
- Unique referral codes
- UTM parameters
- Attribution rules (last-touch, first-touch)
- Multi-touch attribution

### 56.6 — Co-marketing Opportunities (3 أيام)
- Joint webinars
- Case studies
- Press releases
- Conference booth sharing

### 56.7 — Partner Onboarding (5 أيام)
- Application
- Approval workflow
- Welcome kit
- Training resources
- 30/60/90 day check-ins
