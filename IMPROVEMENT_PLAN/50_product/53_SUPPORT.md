# 53 — Support System | نظام الدعم الفني

## 🟠 الأولوية: عالي

## 🔍 الفجوات
- لا ticketing system
- لا live chat
- لا escalation
- لا SLA tracking

## 🎯 الخطة

### 53.1 — Help Desk Setup (5 أيام)
**الخيارات:**
- Intercom (موصى)
- Crisp (أرخص، عربي جيد)
- Help Scout
- Zendesk (للـ enterprise)

### 53.2 — Live Chat (3 أيام)
- In-app widget
- Bot for FAQs (معتمد على Knowledge Base + RAG)
- Hand-off to human agent
- Office hours / availability

### 53.3 — Ticketing (5 أيام)
- Multi-channel (email, chat, in-app)
- Priority levels
- Categories
- Tags
- Assignment rules
- Auto-responses

### 53.4 — SLA Tracking (4 أيام)
| Plan | First Response | Resolution |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Starter | 24h | 5 days |
| Pro | 4h | 2 days |
| Enterprise | 1h | 1 day |

### 53.5 — Escalation Workflow (3 أيام)
- L1 (general support) → L2 (technical) → L3 (engineering)
- Auto-escalation after timeout
- Manager notifications

### 53.6 — Knowledge Base Integration (3 أيام)
- AI-suggested articles before ticket creation
- Auto-link relevant docs

### 53.7 — Customer Health Score (5 أيام)
- Login frequency
- Feature usage
- Support tickets
- Payment status
- Predict churn

### 53.8 — Office Hours / Status Page (3 أيام)
- statuspage.namasoft.com
- Incident history
- Scheduled maintenance
- Subscribe to updates

### 53.9 — Customer Success Reps (للـ Enterprise) (4 أيام)
- Dedicated CSM per account
- Quarterly business reviews
- Health monitoring
- Upsell opportunities

## 📊 KPIs
| KPI | قبل | بعد |
|-----|-----|-----|
| First response time | غير مقاس | < 4h |
| Resolution time | غير مقاس | < 24h |
| CSAT score | غير مقاس | > 4.5/5 |
| Tickets resolved on first contact | غير مقاس | > 70% |
