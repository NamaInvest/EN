# 2C — Financial Reporting | التقارير المالية

## 🟠 الأولوية: عالي | الاكتمال: 50%

## 🔍 الموجود
- Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow basic
- Trial Balance
- Recharts للـ visualizations

## 🔴 الفجوات
- لا Comparative reports (vs prior period/year)
- لا Multi-period (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 side-by-side)
- لا Budget vs Actual
- لا Variance Analysis
- Notes to Financial Statements غير موجود
- لا IFRS-compliant disclosures
- لا Consolidation reports
- لا Cash Flow Indirect method
- لا Statement of Changes in Equity
- لا Segment Reporting
- لا Interim Reports (quarterly)
- لا Custom Report Builder

## 🎯 الخطة

### 2C.1 — Comparative & Multi-Period Reports (5 أيام)
- Current vs Prior Period
- Current Year vs Prior Year
- Quarterly side-by-side
- YTD vs Full Year forecast

### 2C.2 — Budget vs Actual (6 أيام)
- Variance analysis (favorable/unfavorable)
- % variance
- Drill-down to JE level
- Auto-generated commentary (AI)

### 2C.3 — Notes to Financial Statements (8 أيام)
- Auto-generated notes per IFRS
- Significant accounting policies
- Critical estimates
- Related party disclosures
- Subsequent events
- Notes per line item

### 2C.4 — Cash Flow Indirect Method (4 أيام)
- From P&L + BS changes
- Operating, Investing, Financing
- Reconciliation to direct method

### 2C.5 — Statement of Changes in Equity (3 أيام)
- Per equity component
- Reconciliation
- IFRS format

### 2C.6 — Segment Reporting (IFRS 8) (5 أيام)
- Operating segments
- By geography, product, customer type
- Inter-segment elimination
- Reconciliation to consolidated

### 2C.7 — Custom Report Builder (10 أيام)
- Drag-drop fields
- Calculations & formulas
- Filters & groupings
- Charts & visualizations
- Save & share

### 2C.8 — Scheduled Reports (4 أيام)
- Daily / Weekly / Monthly cadence
- Auto-email to recipients
- PDF + Excel formats
- Distribution lists

### 2C.9 — XBRL Export (5 أيام)
- لـ ZATCA quarterly filings
- لـ stock exchange (لو شركة مساهمة)

### 2C.10 — Real-time Dashboards (6 أيام)
- KPI tiles
- Drill-down
- Filters (period, branch, segment)
- Mobile-friendly

## 📊 KPIs
| KPI | قبل | بعد |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Reports generation time | غير محدد | < 10s |
| Notes to FS | لا | كامل IFRS |
| Custom reports | لا | self-service |
| Scheduled reports | لا | للـ executives |
