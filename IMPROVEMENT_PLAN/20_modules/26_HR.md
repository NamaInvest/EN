- Employee, Attendance, Vacation
- Basic HR

## 🔴 الفجوات
- لا Employee Onboarding workflow
- لا Org Chart visualization
- Performance Management ضعيف
- لا Training & Development
- لا Self-Service Portal للموظفين
- لا Recruitment / ATS
- Time & Attendance بدائي
- Leave Management ضعيف
- لا Career Path planning
- لا Succession Planning

- لا Document Management (إقامات، تأمينات)

## 🎯 الخطة

### 26.1 — Onboarding (5 أيام)
- Pre-boarding checklist
- Document collection
- IT setup
- Training assignments
- First-week schedule

### 26.2 — Org Chart (3 أيام)
- Visual تركيبة الشركة
- Drag-drop reorg
- Reporting hierarchy

### 26.3 — Performance Management (8 أيام)
- Goals (OKRs / SMART)
- 360 feedback
- Quarterly reviews
- Calibration sessions
- Rating distribution

### 26.4 — Training (5 أيام)
- Course catalog
- Enrollment
- Completion tracking
- Certificates

### 26.5 — Employee Self-Service (10 أيام)
- View payslips, leave balance
- Request leave/approval
- Update personal info
- Download documents (salary cert, employment cert)
- Chat with HR

### 26.6 — Recruitment / ATS (10 أيام)
- Job postings
- Applicant tracking
- Interview scheduling
- Offer letters
- Background checks

### 26.7 — Time & Attendance (6 أيام)
- Biometric integration
- Mobile check-in (geofencing)
- Shift management
- Overtime tracking
- Late/Early departure

### 26.8 — Leave Management (5 أيام)
- Saudi labor law compliance
- Annual, sick, hajj, maternity, paternity
- Accrual rules
- Carryover rules
- Approval workflow

### 26.9 — Document Management + Expiry (4 أيام)
- Iqama, passport, license
- Auto-alerts 60/30/7 days before expiry
- Renewal workflow

### 26.10 — Saudization (3 أيام)
- Nitaqat tracking
- Saudi vs Non-Saudi ratios
- Compliance reports
- Hiring suggestions
