# Financial Invariants

## Never Broken Rules

### 1. Journal Balance
Debit total must always equal Credit total.

### 2. Posted Journal Entries
Posted journals are immutable.

### 3. ZATCA Cleared Invoices
Cannot be edited or deleted.

### 4. Inventory Integrity
Inventory quantity can never become inconsistent with stock movements.

### 5. Payment Allocation
Allocated payment amount cannot exceed payment amount.

### 6. Tenant Isolation
Financial transactions can never cross tenants.

### 7. Currency Integrity
All journals must preserve base currency balancing.

### 8. Audit Integrity
Financial audit logs are append-only.

### 9. Sequential Numbering
Official invoices must preserve numbering sequence.

### 10. Period Lock
No transactions allowed in closed periods.
