# Sales & POS Options Rule

**LAW / SYSTEM RULE:**
In the sales settings/options (خيارات المبيعات), any configuration, option, or setting that is applied must be universally applied across all of the following modules:
1. `pos` (Point of Sale)
2. `restaurant-pos` (Restaurant Point of Sale)
3. `sales` (General Sales)

This means these three modules share the exact same behavioral settings and business logic configurations when it comes to sales options.
